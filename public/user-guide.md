# Poop Clan Server HQ — Quick User Guide

For James and Tyler: a shared planning room for your Rust game server.

1. **Start together.** Sign in with your invited account. On Overview, select **Load starting plan** if it is shown. This adds suggested tasks and draft handbook pages without replacing your work. Use **Workspace settings** in the footer to record the game, mission, and server details.
2. **Shape the server.** Agree on your audience, PvP/PvE approach, wipe policy, core features, and what makes your Rust server distinctive. Capture the discussion and decisions before committing to plugins or promotion.
3. **Share ideas.** Open **Ideas & decisions → Submit an idea**. Give the idea a title, description, category, and phase. Open it again to discuss it. Approve, request changes, defer, or decline with a decision comment. Approval creates a linked task automatically.
4. **Assign work.** Open a task in **Master plan**, select **Edit**, and enter **James** or **Tyler** in **Owner**. Add priority, target date, and a **Done when** criterion. Select dependencies when other work must finish first. Use **To do → In progress → Done**, or **Blocked** with an explanation of the help needed. Complete dependencies before marking a task done.
5. **Communicate in the right place.** Use **Team chat** for quick conversation. Use a task's **Discussion** for its questions and implementation details. Use **Note board** for research, meeting notes, and handoffs. Pin important notes to show them on Overview. Saved records refresh every few seconds while the page is open.
6. **Leave a handoff.** Before stopping, record what changed, what you checked, what remains uncertain, and the next action. Choose **Handoff** as the note type so it is easy to recognize.
7. **Build your handbook.** Open **Admin handbook**, choose a page, and select **Edit page**. Use **Draft**, **Verified**, or **Needs review**. Record verification dates after testing. The history button shows earlier versions. Keep the current plugin details in **Plugin registry** and values/reasons/recovery steps in **Settings & rationale**. An approval or registry edit does not operate the game server.
8. **Track costs.** Use **Budget** for hosting, plugin licenses, AI services, advertising, and income. Select direction, amount, frequency, and status. Totals include planned entries and count each entry once; they are planning figures, not a bank balance.
9. **Connect your ChatGPT partners.** Open **ChatGPT connection** for the endpoint and instructions. Each person connects from their own eligible account and completes authorization. Test a project summary, then save a clearly labeled test note and confirm it appears. Current tools read the plan and handbook and submit ideas or notes; assign and update tasks in the dashboard. Private ChatGPT conversations are not automatically imported.
10. **Keep your work recoverable.** Use **Archive** in the footer to restore archived items. **Export workspace** downloads all saved records, full chat history, and handbook revisions as JSON. Automatic import is not included. Store passwords, tokens, recovery codes, and private player information separately from general project notes and source code.

## If someone edits the same item

The app checks versions before saving. If a newer version appears, your open draft stays in the editor. Review the latest version, merge the details you need, acknowledge that you have merged your draft, and save. Unsaved drafts are not durable shared records; save before closing the page.

## A useful weekly routine

Review Overview and pinned notes. Discuss pending ideas. Choose three priorities, assign one owner per task, unblock work, review costs, and leave the next action clear.

## Help controls

Select **Help & guide** in the header to replay the quick-start walkthrough. Section tips can be dismissed individually or turned off; **Show all section tips again** restores them. These help preferences are saved only for your account in the browser you are using. Download this guide from the walkthrough whenever you need a copy.

## Button sounds

Buttons play a short synthesized fart sound when clicked. Use the speaker control in the header to mute or enable sounds. Your sound preference is remembered in this browser. Sounds start with a button click; browser audio support and device volume still apply.
